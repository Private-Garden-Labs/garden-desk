description: Legal document set completeness. Load after document-review.

Map requested topics to reviewed files/sections. Status: complete, partial, missing, unreadable, conflicting, duplicate, or out of scope. One related file does not establish completeness.

Check referenced schedules, exhibits, consents, approvals, amendments, notices, and signatures. Record each issue's entity, document, date, parties, subject, source fact, supplied threshold, and possible effect.

Table: topic; status; source fact; location; supplied threshold; check. End with open requests. Do not decide legal effect, validity, breach, control, or ownership.
