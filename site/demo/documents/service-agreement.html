SERVICES AGREEMENT

No. BA-2026-114, dated 3 November 2026

This Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between:

Brightfield Analytics SRL, registered in Cluj-Napoca, Romania, company number J12/4471/2019, represented by Ana Petrescu, Managing Director (the "Supplier"); and

Cedar Lane Retail Ltd, registered in Bristol, United Kingdom, company number 11873520, represented by Thomas Hale, Finance Director (the "Client").

1. Services

1.1 The Supplier will design, build and maintain a sales reporting dashboard for the Client's 14 stores, as described in Annex A (the "Services").

1.2 The Supplier will perform the Services with reasonable skill and care, using suitably qualified staff.

2. Term

2.1 This Agreement starts on 1 December 2026 and continues for a period of 12 months, ending on 30 November 2026 (the "Term").

2.2 The parties may extend the Term by written agreement signed by both parties.

3. Fees

3.1 The Client will pay the Supplier the following fees, excluding VAT:

ItemDescriptionAmount (EUR)
1Discovery and requirements workshop4,500.00
2Dashboard design and build9,800.00
3Staff training (two sessions)1,200.00
4Support and maintenance, 12 months at EUR 350.00 per month4,200.00

3.2 The total fee for the Services is EUR 18,700.00, excluding VAT.

3.3 Items 1 to 3 are invoiced on completion. Item 4 is invoiced monthly in arrears.

4. Payment

4.1 The Client will pay each correct invoice within 30 days of receipt, by bank transfer to the account stated on the invoice.

4.2 Late payments carry interest at 0.05% per day of delay, up to the amount of the unpaid invoice.

5. Confidentiality

5.1 Each party will keep the other party's confidential information secret and use it only to perform this Agreement. This obligation continues for 3 years after the Agreement ends.

6. Liability

6.1 Each party's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the total fee stated in Section 3.2, except for fraud or wilful misconduct.

7. Termination

7.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience by giving the other party 60 days' written notice.

7.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately if the other party commits a material breach and does not remedy it within 15 days of written notice.

7.3 During the notice period of 30 days set out in Section 7.1, the Supplier will continue to provide the Services and the Client will pay for all work completed.

8. General

8.1 This Agreement is governed by Romanian law. Any dispute will be settled by the competent courts of Cluj-Napoca.

8.2 This Agreement is signed in two original copies, one for each party.
