What sponsorship pays for

  • The time to keep the model, the sandbox, and the app current.
  • Code-signing certificates for macOS and Windows, so downloads open without warnings.
  • Test hardware: Macs and PCs, so every release is verified on the machines people actually own.

No strings

There is nothing to unlock. Every feature is free for everyone, sponsored or not. Sponsorship does not buy a feature, a release date, or a place in the roadmap. You keep a private tool private, for everyone.

How this list is kept

Backers are listed by name. Sponsors add a logo and a link. Company sponsors appear at the top of this page. Sponsors and company sponsors are also listed in the project README, and both places are updated by hand with each release, so a new entry can take a few days to appear.

Being listed is optional. Sponsor privately, or write to developer@gardendesk.ai and your entry is removed. Nothing on this page is tracked, and no sponsor data is collected here.
