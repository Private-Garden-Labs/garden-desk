# Distribution Agreement

**Date:** 15 January 2027

**Parties:** Lindqvist Kaffee GmbH, Hafenstrasse 12, 20457 Hamburg, Germany ("Supplier"), and Maison Arôme SARL, 8 rue des Halles, 69001 Lyon, France ("Distributor").

## 1. Products and territory

1.1 Supplier appoints Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the Lindqvist Barista espresso machines listed in Schedule A (the "Products") in France and Belgium (the "Territory").

1.2 Distributor shall not actively market or sell the Products to customers in Spain, which Supplier has allocated exclusively to another distributor.

1.3 Distributor may resell the Products to customers in any country, including the Russian Federation and Belarus. Schedule A lists the Products at a resale value of EUR 1,450 per machine.

## 2. Prices and resale

2.1 Supplier sells the Products to Distributor at the prices in Schedule A.

2.2 Distributor shall not resell any Product below the retail price list Supplier issues each quarter. Supplier may stop deliveries to Distributor if Distributor sells below that list.

2.3 Distributor shall sell the Products only from its physical shops. Distributor shall not sell the Products through its own website, online marketplaces, or any other online channel.

## 3. Payment

3.1 Distributor shall pay each invoice within 120 days of receipt.

3.2 No interest and no compensation for recovery costs shall be due for late payment.

## 4. Customer data

4.1 Distributor shall send the names, addresses, and purchase histories of all end customers to any service provider Supplier chooses, in any country, without written processing terms and without informing the customers.

## 5. Non-competition

5.1 During this Agreement, Distributor shall not sell espresso machines that compete with the Products. This obligation lasts for three years from the date of this Agreement.

## 6. Consumer sales terms

6.1 Distributor shall use the following terms for every sale to consumers, including sales by telephone:

(a) All sales are final. Consumers may not withdraw from a purchase or return a Product that works.

(b) Any dispute with a consumer shall be brought exclusively before the courts of Hamburg, Germany, whatever the consumer's place of residence.

(c) The legal guarantee for defects is limited to six months from delivery.

## 7. Term and termination

7.1 This Agreement starts on 1 March 2027 and ends on 28 February 2026, unless terminated earlier under clause 9.

7.2 If Distributor breaches clause 2, Distributor shall pay Supplier a contractual penalty of EUR 50,000 for each breach.

## 8. Governing law

8.1 This Agreement is governed by German law. The United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods applies to each sale of Products from Supplier to Distributor.
