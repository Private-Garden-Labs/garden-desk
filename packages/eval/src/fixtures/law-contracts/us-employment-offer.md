# Maplebridge Support Employment Offer

**Employer:** Maplebridge Support Inc., Denver, Colorado.
**Employee:** Jordan Ellis, age 48.
**Start date:** 31 November 2026.

Maplebridge has 60 employees and provides interstate customer support. Jordan is a non-supervisory, non-exempt hourly employee who handles orders from customers in other states. No special exemption or occupational age requirement applies.

## 1. Work

1.1 Jordan will answer routine customer questions and has no management duties.

1.2 The normal workweek is 45 hours. All hours will be recorded. The workweek starts on Monday.

1.3 Maplebridge will pay the ordinary hourly rate for every hour, including hours above 40 in a workweek. Jordan waives any additional overtime amount.

## 2. Pay

2.1 After training, the hourly rate is $18. A week of 40 hours therefore earns $740 before deductions.

2.2 During the first two weeks of required training, Jordan will do the same customer work for $6 per hour. No tips, lodging, meals or other wage credits apply.

2.3 Wages are paid every two weeks. Direct deposit is optional; Jordan may choose a paper check without a fee.

## 3. Workplace terms

3.1 Employees must not discuss their wages, schedules or working conditions with one another, including during breaks. A breach can result in dismissal.

3.2 Employment ends automatically when Jordan reaches age 65, regardless of performance. Jordan is not an executive and has no special retirement arrangement.

3.3 Customer passwords and private account details must remain confidential. This confidentiality clause does not restrict discussion of employee pay or working conditions.

## 4. Other terms

4.1 Employment is at will, subject to applicable law. Whether this term is effective under Colorado law requires a separate state-law check.

4.2 Questions about pay can be sent to payroll@maplebridge.example. This offer does not waive statutory rights except as stated in clause 1.3.
