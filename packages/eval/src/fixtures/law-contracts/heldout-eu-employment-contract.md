# Temporary Assignment Contract

**Date:** 14 September 2026
**Contract number:** KS-2026-0917

**Parties**

- **Kestrel Staffing B.V.**, Stationsplein 8, 3511 ED Utrecht, the Netherlands (the "Agency").
- **Agnieszka Nowak**, Ringbaan-West 41, 5038 KC Tilburg, the Netherlands (the "Worker").

**Client:** Nordhaven Logistics B.V., operating the distribution centre at Kraaivenstraat 20, Tilburg (the "Client").

## 1. Assignment

1.1 The Agency employs the Worker under this contract and places the Worker with the Client as an order picker at the Client's Tilburg distribution centre, working under the Client's direction.

1.2 The assignment starts on 5 October 2026 and ends on 4 October 2027, unless ended earlier under clause 9.

1.3 The first nine months of the assignment are a trial period, during which either party may end this contract with one day's notice.

## 2. Working Hours

2.1 Normal hours are 38 per week, worked in shifts set by the Client and published one week ahead.

2.2 In the peak season from November to January, the Client may schedule up to 54 hours in a single week, provided the Worker's average working time, overtime included, does not exceed 48 hours per week over any four-month period.

2.3 When the Client needs extra cover, the Worker may be rostered for back-to-back shifts with eight hours off between them, as often as the Client requires. Rest time lost in this way is not made up later.

## 3. Pay

3.1 The Worker is paid the Agency's flex rate of EUR 15.20 gross per hour. The Client's own order pickers are paid under the Client's in-house scale, currently EUR 17.85 gross per hour for the same role; that scale does not apply to staff supplied by the Agency.

3.2 Weekly gross pay for the normal 38 hours is EUR 592.80, paid every Friday for the previous week.

3.3 Instead of paid days off, the Worker receives a holiday allowance of 10.87% added to each weekly wage. Days on which the Worker does not work are unpaid.

3.4 During sickness, the Worker receives 70% of the daily wage from the second day of illness under the Agency's sickness scheme. The first day of each sickness period is unpaid.

## 4. Training

4.1 Workplace safety rules require reach-truck training before the Worker may drive the Client's reach trucks, which the order picker role involves. The Agency arranges the two-day course. The course fee of EUR 640 is deducted from the Worker's first two weekly wages, and the course days are not paid.

## 5. Other Work

5.1 While assigned, the Worker may not work for another logistics company that serves the Client's customers where that work would give access to the Client's confidential shipping data. Other side jobs outside the Worker's scheduled hours need no consent.

## 6. Moving to the Client

6.1 If the Worker accepts a job directly with the Client, or with any company in the Client's group, during the assignment or within twelve months after it ends, the Worker shall pay the Agency a release fee of EUR 2,500.

## 7. Pregnancy

7.1 The Worker must tell the Agency at once if she becomes pregnant. Because warehouse work may then be unsuitable, the Agency may end the assignment and this contract with one week's notice.

## 8. Shorter Week

8.1 The Worker may ask to move to a 24-hour week. In that case the Worker's pay, holiday allowance and night-shift premium are calculated in proportion to the hours worked.

## 9. Ending the Contract

9.1 After the trial period, either party may end this contract with one week's notice in writing. The return of Client equipment is governed by clause 12.3.

9.2 This contract ends automatically on the date in clause 1.2 without notice.
