## 1. Purchase

1.1 Buyer buys an AeroPure Home Care System, made up of an upright vacuum, a carpet shampooer and a room air purifier, with accessories (the "System"). This sale was made in Buyer's living room after a free in-home demonstration by Seller's representative.

1.2 Cash price $3,450.00, less down payment $250.00, leaves an unpaid balance of $3,200.00.

## 2. Truth in Lending Disclosures

2.1 Annual Percentage Rate: 17.90%. Finance Charge: $959.08. Amount Financed: $3,200.00. Total of Payments: $4,259.08.

2.2 Payment schedule: 36 monthly payments of $115.53, the first due on July 22, 2026 and the rest on the 22nd of each following month.

## 3. No Cancellation

3.1 Because the System is demonstrated and unpacked in Buyer's home, all sales are final once this contract is signed, and the down payment is not refundable.

## 4. Payments

4.1 Buyer must enroll in AeroPure AutoPay, which withdraws each monthly payment from Buyer's checking account. Financing under this contract is offered only to AutoPay customers.

4.2 Because Buyer is married, Buyer's spouse must sign as Co-Buyer for this financing to be approved, whatever Buyer's own income and credit history.

## 5. Security

5.1 Seller keeps a security interest in the System until all payments are made.

5.2 As further security, Buyer also gives Seller a lien on the furniture and kitchen appliances already in Buyer's home. Buyer keeps possession of those items.

## 6. Sale of This Contract

6.1 Seller may sell this contract to a finance company. Buyer agrees that any problem with the System is between Buyer and Seller only, and that Buyer will keep paying the finance company in full even if the System does not work.

## 7. Warranty

7.1 Seller gives a written 3-year limited warranty on the motors of the vacuum and the shampooer, set out in Schedule B. Apart from that written warranty, the System is sold AS IS, and Seller makes no implied promise that the System is of average quality or suitable for Buyer's home.

## 8. Collection

8.1 If the account is passed to an outside collection agency, Buyer agrees that the agency may discuss the overdue balance with Buyer's employer, relatives and neighbors, and may call Buyer at work.

8.2 If Buyer is more than 30 days behind, Seller's agents may enter Buyer's home without further notice to take back the System.

## 9. Disputes

9.1 Any dispute arising from this contract, other than a claim under the written warranty in clause 7, will be resolved by binding individual arbitration before one arbitrator under the American Arbitration Association Consumer Arbitration Rules. Seller pays all filing fees above $200. Either party may instead bring an individual claim in small claims court.

## 10. Electronic Signing

10.1 Before signing, Buyer agreed to receive this contract and later notices electronically, after being told what software is needed to open them, how to get paper copies at no charge and how to withdraw that agreement, and after opening a test document sent to Buyer's email address. Buyer signed on Seller's tablet and received a copy by email.
