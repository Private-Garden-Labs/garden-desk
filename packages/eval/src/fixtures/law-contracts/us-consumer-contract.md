# Sunfield Smart Thermostat Purchase and Service Agreement

**Date:** March 3, 2027

**Parties:** Sunfield Home, Inc., 410 Congress Avenue, Austin, Texas ("Sunfield"), and the customer named in the order summary ("Customer").

## 1. Purchase

1.1 Customer buys one Sunfield T3 smart thermostat (the "Device") for $249, as shown in the order summary in Section 9.

1.2 Sunfield and its authorized dealers have agreed with ThermaCore LLC, Sunfield's main competitor, that neither company will sell a smart thermostat below $249 in 2027.

## 2. Payment plan

2.1 Customer may pay in 12 monthly installments of $25 each, which include a finance charge. Sunfield will tell Customer the annual percentage rate only if Customer asks in writing.

2.2 Payment plans are not available to Customers who were born outside the United States.

## 3. Monitoring subscription

3.1 The Device includes a free 30-day trial of Sunfield Monitoring. After the trial, the subscription renews automatically every year at Sunfield's then-current price, charged to the card Customer used for the Device. This term appears only in this Agreement, which Customer accepts by opening the box.

3.2 To cancel the subscription, Customer must send a notarized letter by certified mail to Sunfield's Austin office at least 60 days before the renewal date.

## 4. Limited warranty

4.1 Sunfield warrants the Device against defects in materials and workmanship for one year from delivery.

4.2 This warranty is void if the Device is installed by anyone other than a Sunfield-certified technician, or if Customer uses any sensor or part not sold by Sunfield.

## 5. Reviews

5.1 Customer agrees not to post any negative review of Sunfield or the Device on any website. Customer shall pay Sunfield $500 for each negative review Customer posts.

## 6. Disputes

6.1 Any dispute shall be resolved by individual binding arbitration under the Federal Arbitration Act. Customer waives any right to bring or join a class action.

6.2 This Agreement is governed by Texas law. Late installments carry a fee of 10% per month.

6.3 If Customer ends the subscription in its first year, Customer shall pay an early termination fee of $300.

## 7. Electronic notices

7.1 Customer consents to receive all notices electronically at the email address in the order summary. Customer may withdraw this consent at any time and may request free paper copies.

## 8. Changes

8.1 Sunfield may change the price of the Device after delivery by notice under Section 7.
