## 1. The Service

1.1 Lumenvault provides online photo and video storage, automatic backup from up to six family devices, and editing tools through its app and website, offered in English and Portuguese (the "Service").

1.2 The subscription starts on 3 March 2026 and continues month to month, subject to clause 8.

## 2. Price and Payment

2.1 The Family Plan costs EUR 9.99 per month, VAT included, charged in advance to your card.

2.2 The total cost of the first year is therefore EUR 129.88 (12 x EUR 9.99).

2.3 The Vault Plus extended version history add-on (EUR 2.99 per month) is shown as selected on the checkout page and is added to your plan unless you untick it before paying.

2.4 We may change the monthly fee at any time and at our discretion. The new fee is charged from your next billing date, and we do not send separate notice of the change.

## 3. Changing Your Mind

3.1 You may change your mind and cancel within 14 days of the start date by using the Cancel button in Account Settings or by emailing support@lumenvault.example.

3.2 If you asked us to switch on your library straight away and you then cancel within that 14-day window, we keep a share of the first monthly fee that matches the number of days the library was available to you, and refund the rest.

3.3 A one-off account closure charge of EUR 25 is deducted from any refund made under clause 3.1.

5.1 The Service is offered as it stands from time to time. Lumenvault does not promise that backups will be complete, that the Service will be available, or that it will suit any purpose. Whatever the fault, your only remedy is a credit of up to one month's fee against future invoices.

## 6. Your Files and Personal Information

6.2 By creating an account you agree that we may share your name, email address and library usage statistics with selected partners for their own advertising. This permission is part of the subscription and cannot be withdrawn while the subscription continues.

## 8. Ending the Agreement
