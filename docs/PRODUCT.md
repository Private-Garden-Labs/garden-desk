# Product

Garden Desk is a free desktop agent for private work with local files. It is for individuals and small offices, such as accountants, lawyers, consultants, and medical administrators, who need work done without uploading files or configuring models.

## Principles

- **Local and offline.** No account, cloud dependency, silent cloud fallback, or telemetry.
- **Outcome first.** People ask for results. The ordinary interface never shows RAG, embeddings, context windows, quantization, providers, or tool calling.
- **Hardware-aware defaults.** The app chooses the model and the context size. People do not configure models.
- **Safe by construction.** Selected folders stay read-only. Results are files that people review and choose to save. Consequential host actions need approval.
- **Verifiable privacy.** Network isolation comes from a microVM with no network device, not from filtering.

## Community And Paid

- The community app is Apache-2.0 and free for personal and commercial use. It has no usage limits and is never a restricted demo.
- Paid offerings may add support, installation, certified computers, and office controls such as accounts, roles, backup, and fleet management. They stay separate modules and never limit the community app.
- A purchased version keeps working permanently. Renewals pay for support and updates, not for access.
